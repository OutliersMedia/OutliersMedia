## SCHEDULE FLEXIBILITY

### How It Works

1. **You choose your plan start date** (any date)
2. **The 7-day posting cycle begins on that date**
3. **Day 1 = Your chosen start date**
4. **Pattern repeats throughout your subscription**

### Example

If you start on **October 15th**:
- October 15 = Day 1 (Post 7:00 PM)
- October 16 = Day 2 (Stories)
- October 17 = Day 3 (Reel + Post)
- ... and so on
- October 22 = Day 7 (Post 11:00 AM)
- October 23 = Day 1 again (Pattern repeats)
